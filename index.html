FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is my audio really private?

Yes. Recording and transcription happen entirely on your Mac - the audio never touches a server, because there is no server. The one online step is optional AI notes: they run through your own Claude account, and you can see exactly which files Claude reads.

Does a bot join my meetings?

No. TNN captures the audio playing on your Mac, so nothing joins the call. No meeting links, no “recording assistant” in the participant list, nothing for the other side to notice.

Do I need a Claude subscription?

Not for the core: recording, transcription, live notes, dictation and your archive work without any account. AI-written notes and the MCP connection use your own Claude Pro or Max subscription - no API keys, no extra AI fees from us.

Where do my files live?

In a folder you choose (by default ~/Documents/TNN). Every meeting is one plain Markdown file: your notes, the transcript and the AI notes together. Delete the app tomorrow - the files are still yours.

What is MCP, in one sentence?

A standard that lets Claude use apps as tools - TNN’s MCP server lets Claude list your meetings, read notes and transcripts, run your skills, and save the results back into the meeting file. Setup is one click in Settings.

Which meeting apps does it work with?

All of them. Zoom, Meet, Teams, Slack huddles, a phone call on speaker, a conversation in the room - if your Mac can hear it, TNN can transcribe it.

Which languages are supported?

TNN detects the language automatically - even when a meeting switches mid-sentence. Built on Whisper, which handles dozens of languages; we use it daily in English and Polish.

What do I need to run it?

A Mac with Apple Silicon on macOS 13 or later. The transcription model downloads once (~600 MB) and runs on the Neural Engine - no internet needed after that.
